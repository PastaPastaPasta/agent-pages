What it is. PastaPastaPasta/dashwallet-desktop is DashPay on the desktop: a wallet for macOS, Linux and Windows with a Rust engine on Platform's platform-wallet library, reached through UniFFI from SwiftUI and SwiftCrossUI screens, plus a Tauri shell and the dwcli command line. Its roadmap runs engine foundations (E0), DashPay (DP1–DP6), then masternodes and governance for wallet holders (MG).

How work lands. One item at a time, each on its own dw/… branch, merged with --no-ff into main under the desktop merge bar, then main CI. The landing owner is ; the work train order is .
